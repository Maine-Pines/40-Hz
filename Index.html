Using it on your phone
  • You can lock the phone once the session starts. It plays like a podcast, shows on the lock screen with pause and play, and stops by itself at the end.
  • Open this page in Safari or Chrome for that. Some in-app browsers, including the one inside the Claude app, may stop sound on lock.
  • A call or another app's audio pauses the session. The timer counts only minutes actually played.
  • If you hear nothing, check the media volume and that another app is not holding the audio.
  • Headphones or the phone speaker both work. This is not a binaural beat.
  • The sound is not phase-locked to your LED display. Both run at 40 Hz independently.
  • Stay awake and attentive. The 40 Hz brain response is strongest in wakefulness.
